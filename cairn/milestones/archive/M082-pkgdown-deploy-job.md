# M082: The pkgdown deploy runs in its own job, and only that job can write or queue

**Status:** done (2026-10-18, PR #84 https://github.com/jmgirard/rlmstudio/pull/84)

**Goal:** A run of `pkgdown.yaml` that does not deploy the site can neither cancel a queued deploy nor get write access to the repository.

**Outcome:** `.github/workflows/pkgdown.yaml` has two jobs. `build` runs under the workflow's `permissions: read-all` and uploads `docs` as the artifact `site`, with `include-hidden-files: true` so `.nojekyll` survives, `retention-days: 1`, and `overwrite: true`. `deploy` has `needs: build`, the M080 deploy condition as its job-level `if:`, and `contents: write`, the only write grant in the file. Its steps are a checkout, then a download of `site` into `docs`, then the gh-pages deploy action with its old inputs. A workflow-level group, `pkgdown-${{ (<deploy condition>) && 'deploy' || github.run_id }}`, puts each deploying run in `pkgdown-deploy`, one at a time, and gives every other run its own group. Manual run 39150570451 on the branch built the site and skipped the deploy. After the merge, push run 39154857749 on `main` built and deployed, and `gh-pages` moved to 36fc532 with `.nojekyll` present. This milestone absorbed the M080 review findings F1 and F2 candidate row.

**Decisions:** none. The plan chose a separate `deploy` job over a personal access token for one step, and the gh-pages branch deploy over `actions/deploy-pages`. Review pass 1 amended AC2 and AC3, which moved the group from the `deploy` job to the workflow level. The reason was that a job-level group let an older build deploy last, and the docs do not say whether a skipped job takes its group.

**Review:** Two fan-outs of three fresh reviewers. Pass 1 sent 3 findings back for the amendment and fixed 4 (retention, overwrite on re-run, two comments). Pass 2 fixed 3 comment findings and sent diff-bug #3 and #6 to the "pkgdown queue edges" candidate row. Across the passes, 29 findings were reported, 9 rejected and 8 noted. CI on PR #84 was first red on `test-mock-http-helper.R:184`, a test that was already red on `main`. Hotfix PR #85 fixed that test first, and `main` was merged into the branch before the merge. Two lessons were added: one on the workflow-level group and hidden artifact files, and one on curl error text across libcurl builds. No lesson was retired.
